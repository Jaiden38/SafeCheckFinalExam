FLOOD PREPAREDNESS CHECKLIST: FLOOD

A flood is a natural disaster that occurs when water overflows onto normally dry land. It is commonly caused by heavy rainfall, overflowing rivers, typhoons, and clogged drainage systems. Flooding can damage homes, roads, and properties, displace families, and disrupt transportation and daily activities. It can also contaminate drinking water and create health risks for affected communities. To stay safe during floods, people should monitor weather updates, prepare emergency supplies, avoid walking or driving through floodwaters, and follow evacuation instructions from local authorities.
